# Draft reply to App Review (guidelines 4.3 and 4.2.6)

NOT SENT. Apple's rejection offers this reply as an alternative to resubmitting. Use it only if the owner decides to.
The owner must confirm every answer first, especially the ones marked CONFIRM.

1. What it does: A one-screen arcade game. Bubbles drift toward a glowing line across the screen. You split them with taps and swipes, and you can drag off the line to "snap" it, flinging nearby bubbles away, with a recharge. Letting a bubble cross costs you in proportion to its weight. It gives short, skill-based play sessions with no accounts, ads or network use.

2. Intended user: Families and casual players, including kids playing with a parent, and two people sharing one device. It's rated 4+ but isn't listed in the Kids Category.

3. Gap it addresses: The snap mechanic, weight-based crossing costs, and a shared arena where what one player misses lands on the other. On iPhone Duo the line sits on the physical fold. CONFIRM: the owner should only claim what they've seen in comparable apps.

4. Beta testing: Playtesting with the owner's kids and the owner, then TestFlight. Feedback applied to the build: a way to win shields back; tiny bubbles pop free on the line; a slower ramp starting from one bubble; scoring and per-object physics (from the kids); humans couldn't clear levels 6 and up, which led to swiping, chain pops and staged arrivals. CONFIRM: internal TestFlight had one tester. The public beta was only just approved, so don't imply wider testing unless it has happened by then.

5. Standalone or suite: Standalone. The account also has Cold Gas, 17-0: Fantasy Football, Moon Club and Hearth, which are different products.

6. Could it be part of another app: No. It's a separate game with no overlap in what those apps do. CONFIRM.

7. Shared code or assets with other apps on the account: A file comparison found no identical Swift or Metal source files with Cold Gas, Moon Club, 17-0 or Hearth. Art is drawn in code in this app's own package. Release scripts outside the binary were adapted from Moon Club. CONFIRM: the comparison only catches identical files, not similar ones.

8. Third-party code: No third-party SDKs or content libraries in the binary; only Apple frameworks.

9. Client or third party: No. The owner's own concept, submitted under their own account. CONFIRM AND DECIDE: the app was written with an AI coding assistant, directed by the owner. It wasn't made from a commercial template, but the owner should decide how to describe this honestly, since 4.2.6 is about app generation services.
