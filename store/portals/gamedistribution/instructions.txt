Keep the bubbles off the glowing line in the middle. Tap or click a bubble to split it, or swipe across bubbles to slice them all. Big ones that cross the line fill your meter, and a full meter costs one of your three shields. Tiny ones pop on the line for free.

Drag off the line to snap it and fling nearby bubbles back (it recharges in 7 seconds). Catch the guest stars for points and a gift. Clear every bubble to win the level.

Two players: lay a touch screen flat between you. Each guards one half.

Esc pauses.
