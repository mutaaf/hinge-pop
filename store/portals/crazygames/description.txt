Hinge Pop is an arcade game about one glowing line. Bubbles drift across the screen, and the line down the middle, the hinge, is yours to guard. Tap a bubble to split it into two smaller ones, or swipe to slice through every bubble in your path. Big ones that cross the hinge cost you. Tiny ones pop on it for free.

Snap the Hinge: drag off the line and it cracks like a whip, flinging nearby bubbles back. Then it needs 7 seconds to recharge, so pick your moment.

Four sizes: smaller bubbles are faster and worth more, from 100 to 500 points. Pop a tiny one and its neighbours go too. Keep tapping to build a combo up to x10, and clear a level with no crossings for a 25% bonus.

A big bubble that crosses fills your overload meter, smaller ones add less, and a full meter costs a shield. You have three. Ten taps in a row without a crossing win one back.

The levels never end. They come in chapters of ten, each tenth level is a breather, and the arena changes as you go.

Two players: lay a phone or tablet flat between you and sit at each end. Each of you guards one half, and what you miss lands on your rival. Last shield standing wins.

A short guided tour teaches the game on your first play. Sound is off until you turn it on in Settings.
