# Hinge Pop 0.1.1: Great Scott, we have visitors

Here's what to test:

- Snap the hinge: drag off the glowing line and it cracks like Indiana Jones's whip, flinging bubbles back, then recharges. Does it feel great? Is 7 seconds the right wait?
- Eight guest stars now wander through. There's a caped hero, a time traveller who jumps around like the flux capacitor just kicked in, a wizard who says you shall not cross, and a shaded fellow who dodges your first tap like it's slow motion. Do they feel like a fun surprise?
- May the bosses be with you. They shrug off button mashing now. When one glows red, hit it for double damage, or its dash costs a shield. Too hard? Too easy?
- No more winning by watching. A big bubble that gets through costs a whole shield, so Level 1 needs at least one tap.
- Busy levels now arrive in stages. Tell us if later levels feel fair.
- Who you gonna read? Nobody. How to Play is now three pictures and folding sections, and the tour and results are much shorter. Is anything missing?
- It should feel smoother, especially on busy screens. Tell us if you see a stutter, and on which device.
