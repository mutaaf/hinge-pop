# Hinge Pop 0.1.0: the first pop heard round the world

To infinity and beyond, one bubble at a time. Here is what to test:

- Like a Jedi's first lesson, it starts small: one slow bubble. Play the first ten levels and tell us if the ramp feels right.
- Fruit-ninja-level swiping: drag across bubbles to slice them all at once. Does it feel good, and does it let you keep up?
- Pop one tiny bubble and watch its neighbours go, a chain reaction Doc Brown would approve of. Try letting them bunch up first.
- Choose your fighter: Fast, Armored, Magnetic, Repel, Golden and Frozen each introduce themselves when they turn up, and the bosses wait at the end of each chapter.
- Mario Kart rules apply: grab Overcharge, Freeze, Focus, Shield and Chain Burst when they drop.
- Two players, one hinge. Sit across from a rival and tell us who wins.
- Got an iPhone Duo? Open it like a book. The hinge should sit right on the fold. We could only test that on the simulator, so your report matters most.

Our promises: no ads, no accounts, no tracking, and everything stays on your device.
