## More
- What it counts: a level started or finished, its level, score, time and crossings, and how the tour went.
- What it never counts: your name, your location, an advertising id, or anything you type.
- Counts wait until the level is over, so nothing interrupts play.

Here's what to test:

- Like a stat-head in a playoff race, we're counting, quietly. Does anything feel slower or different in play?
- Open Settings. Find Privacy, then Share usage stats. Turn it off and on again. Does it stick after a relaunch?
- Finish a level, then go Home. Everything should feel exactly as before.
