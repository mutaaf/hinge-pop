# Hinge Pop 0.5.1: Pit Stop

## Bubble news
- [bolt.fill] Smoother and cooler. Play does a lot less work per frame, so your battery lasts longer. {play}
- [thermometer.medium] Phone running hot or in Low Power Mode? Effects ease off by themselves to keep it smooth.
- [lock.shield.fill] Your progress is now kept with a backup, so a bad save can't wipe your stars.
- [stethoscope] We can spot crashes and slow levels now. Numbers only, and the same switch turns it off. {settings}

## More
- The score counter, the last-shield throb and Home's drifting bubbles are now cheap to draw.
- Backgrounds are drawn off to the side, so screens no longer hitch when they open.
- When your phone runs low on memory, the game lets go of backdrops it can redraw.
- We threw thousands of random games at the rules to shake out edge cases.

Here's what to test:

- Like a pit crew in a championship finale, we tuned the engine. Does a busy Arcade wave or a boss feel smoother than 0.5.0? Does the phone stay cooler?
- Turn on Low Power Mode and play a level. It should still look good, with a little less sparkle.
- Watch the score while it counts up, and the shields at your last one. Do they look right?
- Rotate, background the app mid-level and come back. Anything frozen or missing?
- Force-quit after finishing a level. Is your progress still there when you reopen?
