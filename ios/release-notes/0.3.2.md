## More
- Wide screens split the long way, so both players always sit at the short ends.
- On iPhone Duo each page is a player's half, whichever way the fold runs.
- Every match ends with a card for each of you, and one big Rematch on the line.
- Pause lives at the end of the line, where both of you can reach it.

Here's what to test:

- Like a table-hockey showdown at a seaside arcade, two players should be able to sit across from each other and read everything. Can you?
- Play two-player with the phone flat in portrait. Does the top player's scoreboard read the right way up from their end?
- Watch a bubble cross the line. Does it spin round to face the other player?
- Turn to landscape, or play on iPad. Do the seats move to the short ends?
- Finish a match. Does each of you see your own result, and does Rematch start again?
