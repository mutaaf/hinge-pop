How to play
  • 100
  • 200
  • 300
  • 500
  • Tap a bubble to split it, or swipe to hit every bubble in a line.
  • Popping a tiny bubble sets off every tiny bubble close to it.
  • A larger bubble that crosses the hinge charges your overload. A full meter costs a shield.
  • The smallest bubbles pop on the hinge for free.
  • Each size has its own weight and bounce. Bubbles knock each other around, and a heavy one can shove a light one into the hinge.
  • Tap 8 times in a row with no crossing to win a shield back.
  • 2 Players: each player owns one side. A crossing charges whoever let it through and lands on the other side.
