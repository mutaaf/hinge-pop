Hinge Pop

Tap a bubble to split it. Tap the smallest size to pop it. A bubble that crosses the hinge splits for free, but charges the overload meter. The smallest size just pops on the hinge, at no cost. Fill the meter and you lose a shield. Tap 8 times in a row with no crossing to win a shield back. Clear every bubble to win.

2 Players: each player owns one side of the hinge. A crossing charges the player who let it through and lands on the other side.
